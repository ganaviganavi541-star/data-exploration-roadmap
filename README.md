# What Drives Life Expectancy Across Countries?

Week 1 Task - Data Exploration Strategy Roadmap | Yuva Intern (Data Science)

## Overview
Planning stage of a Python data exploration project studying which health, economic and education factors are most associated with life expectancy across countries. Full strategy: Week1_Data_Exploration_Roadmap.docx

## Objectives
- Build a clean country-year dataset from public sources
- Describe life expectancy differences by region and over time
- Test relationships with income, schooling, immunisation and mortality
- Communicate findings with clear visualisations

## Hypotheses
1. Higher GDP per capita is linked to higher life expectancy, with diminishing returns.
2. More years of schooling is linked to higher life expectancy.
3. Higher adult/infant mortality is strongly negatively associated with life expectancy.
4. Higher immunisation coverage is linked to higher life expectancy in developing countries.
5. Life expectancy improves over time in all regions, but gaps persist.

## Data Sources
WHO Global Health Observatory, World Bank Open Data, Our World in Data, Kaggle "Life Expectancy (WHO)" dataset

## Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy, Statsmodels, Scikit-learn, Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Phases
1. Data understanding
2. Data cleaning and preparation

4. Hypothesis testing and modelling
5. Visualisation and insights
6. Documentation and delivery

## Risks and Mitigation
Missing data (imputation), inconsistent definitions (cross-checking sources), correlation vs causation (careful wording), outliers (robust statistics), schedule slips (weekly checkpoints).
